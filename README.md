Inspectorio is a supply-chain management software platform that helps brands, retailers, suppliers, and laboratories digitize and optimize production across quality risk management, responsible sourcing & compliance, production management, traceability, and lab-test management. It serves 15,000+ organizations in apparel/footwear, food & beverage, home & furniture, retail, and outdoor/sports. For integrators it exposes REST integration APIs (organization API-key auth via the `apiKey` header) for its Quality Risk Management (Sight), Responsible Sourcing & Compliance (Rise), and File Management domains, plus HMAC-signed outbound webhooks following the Standard Webhooks specification.

**Areas:** Supply Chain · Quality Management · Compliance · Responsible Sourcing · Traceability · Manufacturing · Retail · Lab Testing · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Inspectorio: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
